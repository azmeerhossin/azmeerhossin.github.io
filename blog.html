Information and Communication Technology (ICT) plays a vital role in modern education by transforming the way knowledge is delivered, accessed, and shared. It enhances teaching and learning through interactive tools, digital resources, online collaboration, and virtual classrooms, making education more engaging and accessible. ICT enables students to develop critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and digital literacy skills that are essential for academic success and future careers. It also supports personalized learning by allowing learners to study at their own pace and according to their individual needs. For educators, ICT provides innovative teaching methods, efficient assessment tools, and opportunities for continuous professional development. Furthermore, ICT bridges geographical barriers, enabling access to quality education and global learning communities. As technology continues to evolve, integrating ICT into education prepares students to adapt to changing workplace demands, promotes lifelong learning, and contributes to innovation, economic growth, and sustainable development in the digital age. ...
